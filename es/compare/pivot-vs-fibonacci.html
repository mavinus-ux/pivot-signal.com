Pivot Point vs Fibonacci Retracement

Dos de los métodos de soporte y resistencia más populares, en comparación directa. Probado en EUR/USD, GBP/JPY y XAU/USD.

Veredicto Rápido

Pivot Points son objetivos, calculados automáticamente y confiables para day trading. Fibonacci Retracements son subjetivos, dibujados en swings y útiles para swing trading. Pivot-Signal usa Pivot Points como sistema principal porque no requieren interpretación y funcionan en cualquier marco temporal.

Cuándo usar cada uno

Usar Pivot Points para:

  • Day trading (Daily pivots give 7 levels for the day)
  • Scalping (M15/H1 pivots for quick reactions)
  • Automated trading (Pivots are deterministic — perfect for EAs)
  • When you need a clear, objective entry/exit

Usar Fibonacci para:

  • Swing trading (D1/W1 swings)
  • Retracement entries after a strong move
  • Manual trading where you can spot the swing
  • When you want confluence with other tools (MACD, RSI, etc.)

Por qué Pivot-Signal usa Pivots (no Fibs)

The Pivot-Signal EA was designed around objective, deterministic calculations. This means:

  • Every signal is reproducible — the same input always gives the same output
  • No human judgment required — the EA can run 24/5 without supervision
  • Backtests are reliable — no "where did you draw the swing?" ambiguity

Fibonacci retracements require identifying swing highs and swing lows, which is inherently subjective. Two traders looking at the same chart will draw different Fibonacci levels. That's fine for manual traders, but it makes Fibs a poor fit for an automated EA.

¿Se pueden usar ambos?

Sí. Muchos traders usan Pivot Points para niveles intradía y añaden Fibonacci en el marco semanal para zonas de swing importantes. Si un pivote diario (ej. S1) y un nivel Fibonacci 61,8% coinciden, esa zona se vuelve de alta probabilidad.

Consejo de confluencia: Cuando un nivel Pivot y un nivel Fib están dentro de 10 pips, esa zona ha mostrado históricamente una tasa de reacción 2-3x mayor (basado en backtests Pivot-Signal en EUR/USD 2018-2025).

Cómo Pivot-Signal usa los Pivots

The Pivot-Signal EA calculates Daily, Weekly, and Monthly pivots from the previous period's high, low, and close. It then:

  1. Monitors price action at each pivot level (S1, S2, S3, P, R1, R2, R3)
  2. Places entries when price touches a level and reverses (mean reversion)
  3. Sets TP1, TP2, TP3 at the next 3 pivot levels in trade direction
  4. Sets stop-loss at 10 pips (Pivot) or candle close on opposite side of SuperTrend (ST)
